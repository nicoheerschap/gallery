About Me

Hi, I'm Yun Xie. I'm originally from China and now based in the Netherlands. I started painting and sculpting as a way to escape my daily concerns. Over time this grew into a true passion, one that has led me to participate in numerous exhibitions and art fairs.

For the past decade, I have worked with ceramics, acrylics, and oils on canvas. Although I have some formal training, I am largely self-taught. This independence has allowed me to develop a distinct artistic style, deeply influenced by the rich traditions of my homeland.

My sculptures often depict people in everyday life, capturing the subtle nuances of posture, emotion, and expression. Together with my paintings, I explore the poetic dialogue between movement and stillness, sky and soul. My work reflects themes of spirituality and inner peace, tracing a personal journey between tradition and reinvention, between the tangible and the transcendent.
